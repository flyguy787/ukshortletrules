Wales

Free registration with the Welsh Revenue Authority is mandatory by 31 March 2027. Councils can start a visitor levy (£1.30 per person per night). Licensing is legislated for, with a default start of March 2030. Business rates need 182 days let.

Registration (WRA)In forceFrom 1 Oct 2026, anyone taking bookings of 31 nights or less must register with the Welsh Revenue Authority by 31 Mar 2027. Registration is free and applies even where no levy exists.source
