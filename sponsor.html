Sponsor UK Short-Let Rules

UK Short-Let Rules is a free, independent tracker of the licensing, registration, planning, tax and visitor levy rules for short-term lets and holiday lets across Scotland, England, Wales and Northern Ireland. Every rule links to its official source.

Who reads it

  • UK short-let hosts and holiday-let owners checking what applies to their property
  • People buying or setting up a holiday let, using the licence checker and buyer due-diligence checker
  • Managers and co-hosts keeping up with council-by-council changes, deadlines and levies

Honest numbers

  • Coverage: all four UK nations and 65 council pages, with more added every week
  • Free tools: licence checker, visitor levy map and calculator, 90-day deadlines, occupancy threshold tracker and more
  • Newsletter and council alerts: a small list that is growing. We will share current, exact figures (subscribers, page views) when you get in touch, and we never round up.

What's on offer

Newsletter sponsor

One clearly labelled sponsor slot in the weekly "what changed" email: a short line of text and a link.

Tool sponsor

A labelled "Sponsored by" line on one free tool, such as the licence checker or levy calculator.

Council page sponsor

A labelled sponsor line on a council page, for firms that serve hosts in that area.

Our rules

  • Every sponsorship is clearly labelled as sponsored.
  • Sponsors never affect what we write, and cannot buy changes to rules, sources or rankings.
  • We only take sponsors that are genuinely useful to UK hosts. Sponsors cannot add tracking pixels or third-party ad code.
