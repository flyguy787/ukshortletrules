Sponsor UK Short-Let Rules

UK Short-Let Rules is a free, independent tracker of UK short-term let rules by council: licences, visitor levies, registration, council tax premiums and business rates, plus a free email alert newsletter. We are now taking a small number of founding sponsors.

Who reads it

  • UK short-let and holiday-let hosts checking the rules where their property is
  • In our first week of Google Search data, most searches were hosts looking up their own council's or nation's rules (Dorset, Wales, Northumberland, Aberdeenshire and others)
  • Owners and managers in the middle of a compliance decision: licences, registration, fire safety, levies, tax

Simple terms

  • Every placement is labelled "Sponsored". Sponsors never influence what we publish about rules, councils or deadlines, and we do not endorse sponsors' products.
  • One sponsor per page per category. We may decline sponsors that are a poor fit for hosts.
  • You supply your copy and logo, and we approve them before they go live. Links are marked as sponsored. No tracking pixels or third-party ad code.
  • Invoiced monthly in advance, in GBP. Either side can cancel with 30 days' notice after any minimum term.
  • Each month we send you the real figures for your placements: page views, clicks and subscriber count.
  • The tracker is general information, not legal or tax advice. That disclaimer stays on every page.
