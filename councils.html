City of EdinburghScotlandWhole-city control area (planning likely needed for secondary lets). 5% visitor levy from 24 Jul 2026, capped at 5 nights. First levy returns due by 30 Oct 2026, payment by 13 Nov 2026.✔source
Glasgow CityScotland5% visitor levy from 25 Jan 2027 on the whole stay; applies to bookings made from 1 Apr 2026.✔source
HighlandScotlandBadenoch & Strathspey control area; council has agreed to submit further control area proposals to Ministers (details unverified). Consulting on a £5 per room per night levy until 30 Nov 2026; decision due 10 Dec 2026.✔source
Perth & KinrossScotlandLicence fees, 3-year licence, new or renewal (Policy Statement, 10 Mar 2025). Secondary letting: £350 (1–2 guests), £530 (3–6), £900 (7–10), £1,600 (11+). Home letting/sharing: £175 / £265 / £450 / £800. Variation or transfer £75. Proposed control area (Highland Perthshire + N. Eastern Perthshire) agreed 17 Jun 2026; Ministers' approval on 12 Oct 2026 reported in planning press (UNVERIFIED); not in force until a designation notice is published. Flats already need planning permission to become a short-term let. Visitor levy: draft scheme with fixed-rate options under consultation 19 Aug to 11 Nov 2026, no decision yet (reported £4 per room per night UNVERIFIED). Public register at pkc.gov.uk/article/22511.✔source
Aberdeen CityScotland7% visitor levy on the whole stay from 1 Apr 2027 (bookings made from 1 Oct 2026). No control area. Licence fees from £470.40 (home sharing/letting, 1–2 guests) and £492.80 (secondary letting, 1–2 guests), effective 1 Jan 2026. No temporary licences or exemptions granted.✔source
Dundee CityScotlandNo control area. Visitor levy consultation 31 Aug to 27 Nov 2026 on £5 per room per night or 5% of the room rate (both capped at 7 nights); earliest start 2028/29.✔source
FifeScotlandNo control area in force (council consulted on the principle in 2025). Visitor levy: early engagement only so far; council says the earliest a levy could start is 2028.✔source
Argyll and ButeScotlandConsulting (21 Sep to 15 Nov 2026) on a proposed control area for Mull and Iona; Coll and Tiree; Islay, Jura and Colonsay; and Oban, Lorn and the Inner Isles. Not in force. Visitor levy consultation (flat rate per room per night proposed) closed 5 Oct 2026; no decision yet. 3-year licence from £285.30 (home sharing/letting, up to 4 guests) or £398.95 (secondary letting, up to 4 guests).✔source
StirlingScotland3% visitor levy from 14 Jun 2027 (bookings made from 1 Jan 2027); Stirling residents and nights beyond 7 refunded. No control area. Licence fees from £113 (home letting/sharing, up to 4) and £283 (secondary letting, up to 4), as published for 2025-26.✔source
West DunbartonshireScotland5% visitor levy on the whole stay from 1 Jul 2027 (bookings made and paid for from 1 Oct 2026). No control area. Licence grant or renewal £929.✔source
Greater London (all boroughs)England90 nights per calendar year whole-home cap without planning permission.✔source
CornwallEngland100% council tax second-home premium from 1 Apr 2025, with exceptions (e.g. holiday-only planning conditions).✔source
GwyneddWalesArticle 4 direction (C3 to C5/C6) quashed. Not in force since the Court of Appeal refused appeal on 6 Feb 2026. Visitor levy: consulted May to Jul 2026; council says no decision yet.✔source
CardiffWalesVisitor levy from 1 Apr 2027 (£1.30 per person per night).✔source
PembrokeshireWalesCouncil tax second-home premium of 125% from 1 Apr 2026 (on top of the standard charge), down from 150% in 2025-26. Premium for 2027-28 was due to be set by full Council on 15 Oct 2026; local press reports 125% was kept (UNVERIFIED).✔source
Isle of AngleseyWalesNo visitor levy: Full Council decided on 24 Sep 2026 not to introduce one at present. Council tax second-home premium kept at 100% for 2026-27 (so a second home pays double).✔source
ConwyWalesVisitor levy decision deferred by Full Council on 24 Sep 2026. Council tax second-home premium of 150% (250% of the standard bill) kept for 2026-27 and 2027-28.✔source
CeredigionWalesCouncil tax second-home premium of 150% (250% of the standard bill) since 1 Apr 2025, covering second homes and self-catering units not in business rates. No visitor levy decision found.✔source
PowysWalesCouncil tax second-home premium of 75% (175% of the standard bill), in place since 1 Apr 2023 and unchanged for 2026-27. No visitor levy at this stage; any levy left to the council elected in May 2027, earliest start 2028.✔source
East LothianScotlandLicence needed. No control area, but check planning before applying. Draft 5% visitor levy consulted on (26 Jun to 18 Sep 2026); a 27 Oct 2026 council meeting is asked to approve an independent review before any decision.✔source
MorayScotlandLicence needed (£540 new or renewal from 1 Apr 2026). No control area. Consultation on whether to have a visitor levy at all runs to 31 Oct 2026; no decision made.✔source
Scottish BordersScotlandLicence needed (3-year licence, £417 to £537 by guest numbers). No control area. No visitor levy proposed.✔source
AberdeenshireScotlandLicence needed (3-year licence, £364 to £1,006 by guest numbers and licence type). No control area. No visitor levy scheme proposed yet.✔source
Dumfries and GallowayScotlandLicence needed (3-year licence, £324 to £454 by guest numbers and licence type). No control area. Visitor levy plans dropped in June 2026.✔source
