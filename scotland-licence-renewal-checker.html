Is your Scottish short-term let licence renewal due?

Enter your council and your licence start date. We estimate when your Scottish short-term let licence expires, when to start your renewal, and link to your council's page.

Not legal advice: this is an estimate and an organising aid. Check the expiry date on your licence and check with your council.

How renewal works in Scotland

  • Licence length. A full licence lasts up to 3 years, or a shorter period set by the council. On renewal, a council can grant a longer period if it has published when it will do so. The licence must state its expiry date. Sources: 1982 Act Sch 1 para 8(2), 2022 Order Sch 2 para 10, Part 2 guidance 2.5 to 2.8.
  • Apply before expiry. If you apply to renew before your licence expires, it stays in effect until the council decides (including any appeal period). You can apply at any time before expiry. Sources: 1982 Act Sch 1 para 8(5), Part 1 guidance section 6.
  • If it lapses. If your licence expires before you apply, the Scottish Government says the application is treated as a new one, and you cannot operate until licensed. A council may, if you show good cause, treat a renewal made up to 28 days late as made on time, but that is at its discretion. Sources: Part 1 guidance section 6, 1982 Act Sch 1 para 8(5A).
  • What a renewal checks. That your details are still correct, that you are still a fit and proper person, and that the premises still meet the mandatory conditions. You must display a site notice for 21 days, as for a new application. Fees and additional conditions can change from last time. Sources: Part 1 guidance section 6, 1982 Act Sch 1 para 2.
