Northern Ireland short-let rules: quick answers

Do I need a Tourism NI certificate to let my property in Northern Ireland?

In force. Under the Tourism (NI) Order 1992, all visitor accommodation needs a current Tourism NI certificate after an inspection. Certificates last 4 years. Star grading is voluntary.

Does a holiday let in Northern Ireland pay business rates?

In force. Self-contained self-catering available commercially for 140 days or more a year is non-domestic (rateable).

How are holiday lets taxed in Northern Ireland now the FHL regime has ended?

In force. The FHL regime was abolished UK-wide from April 2025.
