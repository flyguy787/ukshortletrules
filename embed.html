Add the free short-let licence checker to your site

Hosts, letting agents, property managers and bloggers can add our free checker to any website. Visitors pick a nation and council and see whether they need a licence or registration, with links to the official sources.

Embed code

Copy this and paste it into your page's HTML (in WordPress, use a "Custom HTML" block; in Squarespace or Wix, use an "Embed" or "Code" block):

What you get

  • Free to use, no sign-up and no API key.
  • No tracking, cookies, ads or third-party scripts. It loads one small page from our site.
  • Works on mobile, and with keyboards and screen readers (labelled form controls, live result area).
  • Updated automatically when we update our rules data. Every rule links to its official source.
  • Each result has a "Full details at UK Short-Let Rules" link to the matching nation or council page.

Tips

  • Change height if you need more room (results for some councils are long; the frame scrolls).
  • Please keep the credit link under the frame.
  • Prefer a plain link? Use https://ukshortletrules.co.uk/checker.html.
