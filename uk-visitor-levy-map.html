UK visitor levy and tourist tax map 2026-27

Is there a visitor levy in Scotland?

Yes, council by council. Under the Visitor Levy (Scotland) Act 2024 each council decides, and only Edinburgh charges one now: 5% of the accommodation cost (before VAT) on the first 5 consecutive nights, for stays from 24 Jul 2026 (City of Edinburgh Council). Glasgow (5%, from 25 Jan 2027), Aberdeen (7%, from 1 Apr 2027), Stirling (3%, from 14 Jun 2027) and West Dunbartonshire (5%, from 1 Jul 2027) are confirmed, and bookings made before those dates can already be liable; Highland and Dundee are consulting. See the Scotland short-let rules or the Edinburgh council page for details.

Scotland visitor levy: quick answers

Is there a visitor levy in Scotland?

Yes, but only where a council introduces one under the Visitor Levy (Scotland) Act 2024. Edinburgh is the only Scottish council charging now: 5% of the accommodation cost (before VAT) on the first 5 consecutive nights, for stays from 24 Jul 2026.

Which Scottish councils have confirmed a visitor levy?

Glasgow: 5% (net of VAT) on the whole stay from 25 Jan 2027, for bookings made from 1 Apr 2026. Aberdeen: 7% on the whole stay from 1 Apr 2027, for bookings made from 1 Oct 2026. Stirling: 3% from 14 Jun 2027, for bookings made from 1 Jan 2027. West Dunbartonshire: 5% (before VAT) on the whole stay from 1 Jul 2027, for bookings made from 1 Oct 2026.

How do Edinburgh hosts pay the visitor levy?

Through quarterly returns on visitorlevy.scot. The first returns (stays 24 Jul to 30 Sep 2026) are due 1 to 30 Oct 2026, with payment by 13 Nov 2026. Providers keep 2% of the levy collected.

Which Scottish councils are consulting on a visitor levy?

Highland is consulting on £5 per room per night until 30 Nov 2026, with a decision due 10 Dec 2026 and then an 18-month lead-in. Dundee is consulting until 27 Nov 2026 on £5 per room per night or 5% of the nightly room rate, both capped at 7 nights, with an earliest start of 2028/29. Moray is asking whether to have a levy at all, until 31 Oct 2026.
