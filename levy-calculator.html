Visitor levy calculator for short-let hosts

Work out the visitor levy on a booking in Edinburgh, Glasgow, Aberdeen, Stirling, West Dunbartonshire or Cardiff, check whether the advance-booking rules exempt it, and copy a ready-made line for your guest. Rules from each council's published scheme, checked 2026-10-22.

Guest message (copy and paste)

Estimate, not tax advice. Based on the council's published scheme; the accommodation provider is responsible for the correct amount. Check the official source.

Rules used

CouncilRuleOfficial source
City of Edinburgh5% of the accommodation-only cost (net of VAT), first 5 consecutive nights only. Stays from 24 Jul 2026. Bookings paid for (in part or full) before 1 Oct 2025 are exempt. Stays that began before 24 Jul 2026 are not liable.source source
Glasgow City5% of the accommodation-only cost (net of VAT), whole stay. Stays from 25 Jan 2027. Bookings made on or after 1 Apr 2026 are liable.source source
Aberdeen City7% of the accommodation cost, whole stay. Stays from 1 Apr 2027. Exempt only if the booking was made and a payment (any amount, including a deposit) was received before 1 Oct 2026.source source
Stirling3% of the accommodation-only cost (net of VAT). Stays from 14 Jun 2027, bookings made on or after 1 Jan 2027. Charged on every night, but the guest can claim a refund for nights beyond 7 in one booking. Stirling council-tax-registered residents who booked and paid themselves can claim a refund.source source
West Dunbartonshire5% of the accommodation cost before VAT, whole stay. Stays from 1 Jul 2027. Stays booked and paid for (in part or full) before 1 Oct 2026 are not liable.source source
Cardiff£1.30 per person per night (all ages, including babies) for self-catering, holiday lets, B&Bs and hotels; 75p per adult per night in tent pitches or shared hostel rooms (under-18s free there). Stays from 1 Apr 2027; bookings made from 28 Sep 2026 are liable. One booking of 32 nights or more pays no levy.source source source

Percentage levies apply to the accommodation-only part of the price, net of VAT: leave out optional extras like meals, parking or paid laundry. Exempt guests (for example some disability benefit recipients in Scotland) usually pay and then claim a refund from the council. Highland, Dundee and others are still consulting, so they are not in the calculator: see the levy map. Manchester and Liverpool accommodation BID charges are not statutory levies and are not included.
