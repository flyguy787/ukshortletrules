Do I need to register my holiday let in Wales?

Answer 4 quick questions to see if you must register your visitor accommodation with the Welsh Revenue Authority (WRA).

1. Is the accommodation in Wales?

It can be a cottage, flat, lodge, your main home or spare room, B&B, caravan, glamping pod, hostel or pitch.

2. Do you take bookings for overnight stays there?

Even once a year counts (for example, letting your home on Airbnb for a local event). Friends or family staying for free do not count.

3. Are any of those bookings for 31 nights or fewer?

One short booking is enough.

4. Are you responsible for the accommodation (for example, you own or rent it)?

If an agent or management company runs it for you, you are still the one who must register (they can do it on your behalf).
