England short-let rules: quick answers

Is there a short-term let registration scheme in England?

Not yet in force. A mandatory national registration scheme is coming under s.228 of the Levelling-up and Regeneration Act 2023. GOV.UK (updated 2 Oct 2026) now says it is expected to begin in March 2027. No regulations, portal or fee have been published yet.

Is there a new planning use class (C5) for short-term lets in England?

Proposal only. The C5 short-term-let use class was consulted on in 2023 but is not law. Your local planning authority decides whether your letting is a material change of use. Councils can use Article 4 directions.

What is the London 90-night rule?

In force. In Greater London, a whole home can be short-let for up to 90 nights per calendar year without planning permission. Going over that needs permission (Deregulation Act 2015, s.44).

When does a holiday let in England pay business rates instead of council tax?

In force. To be rated as a business, the let must have been available for at least 140 nights and actually let for at least 70 nights in the last 12 months, and you must intend to make it available for 140 nights in the next 12. From 24 Jul 2026 there are easements for owners of 5+ lets on the same property, and for lets sharing land with another business.

Is England bringing in an Overnight Visitor Levy?

Announced, not yet law. On 10 Sep 2026 the government announced that mayors and Foundation Strategic Authority leaders will get the power to introduce an Overnight Visitor Levy, charged as a percentage of the accommodation cost (not a flat fee). Local leaders choose whether to use it and can offer exemptions (e.g. campsites). Accommodation providers would pay it to the authority. A bill will be introduced in due course; no levy applies yet.

How are holiday lets taxed in England now the FHL regime has ended?

In force. The FHL regime was abolished from April 2025. From April 2027, property income (including holiday-let profits) gets its own income tax rates: 22% basic, 42% higher, 47% additional, with finance cost relief given at 22% (GOV.UK, published 26 Nov 2025).

What safety rules apply to holiday lets in England?

In force. Fire safety duties under the Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005, plus gas and electrical safety, an EPC where needed, and insurance (see GOV.UK guidance).
