Recent changes we'd have emailed you about

  • 30 Oct 2026, Wales: Pembrokeshire: the council has confirmed the second-home council tax premium stays at 125% from 1 April 2027 (2027-28), the same as 2026-27. (source)
  • 29 Oct 2026, UK: Budget 2026 (28 Oct 2026): the property income tax rates starting 6 April 2027 will be 2 points higher than previously announced, at 24% basic, 44% higher and 49% additional, in England, Wales and Northern Ireland (was 22/42/47%). (source)
  • 29 Oct 2026, England: Budget 2026: from 1 April 2027, Small Business Rate Relief thresholds for retail, hospitality and leisure properties in England rise from £12,000 to £18,000 (100% relief) and from £15,000 to £21,000 (tapered relief). (source)
