**Subject:** What changed #1: Wales registration deadline, England's register slips to 2027, new Scottish levies

Hi, and welcome to the first issue of UK Short-Let Rules Tracker. Each week: what changed, what's coming, and a link to the official source for every item. It's not legal or tax advice. Always check the source.

**What changed**

- **England: national register delayed.** GOV.UK now says the mandatory short-term-let register is "expected to begin in March 2027" (updated 2 Oct). There's still no portal or fee, so there's nothing to do yet. https://www.gov.uk/guidance/letting-out-a-self-catering-holiday-home-in-england-rules-and-regulations
- **Wales: registration is open.** If you take bookings of 31 nights or less in Wales, you must register with the Welsh Revenue Authority by 31 March 2027. It's free, and you must register even if your council has no visitor levy. https://www.gov.wales/registering-visitor-accommodation-overview
- **England: business-rates tweak.** Owners of 5 or more self-catering lets on the same property, and lets that share land with another business, no longer have to meet the 140/70-night tests (from 24 July). https://www.gov.uk/guidance/apply-for-business-rates-for-a-self-catering-property-in-england
- **Highland: levy consultation open.** The proposal is £5 per room per night for short-term lets, March to November only. The council decides on 10 December. https://www.highland.gov.uk/news/article/17462/highland-council-opens-statutory-public-consultation-on-a-tiered-fixed-amount-visitor-levy-proposal
- **Perth & Kinross: levy consultation open, and a control area agreed.** A draft levy scheme has been under consultation since 19 August. Separately, a control area for Highland Perthshire and north-eastern Perthshire is awaiting approval from Scottish Ministers. https://consult.pkc.gov.uk/communities/proposal-of-short-term-let-control-area/

**Coming up**

- 10 Dec 2026: Highland visitor levy decision
- 25 Jan 2027: Glasgow 5% levy starts (it already applies to bookings made since 1 April 2026)
- 31 Mar 2027: Wales WRA registration deadline
- 1 Apr 2027: Cardiff levy starts (£1.30 per person per night)

**One to check:** if you're in Scotland and pay business rates, you must send your 2026–27 letting evidence (140 nights available, 70 let) to the assessor by 26 May 2027, or you'll be charged council tax for that year. https://www.mygov.scot/non-domestic-rates-guidance/short-term-lets
