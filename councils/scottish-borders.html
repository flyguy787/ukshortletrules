Licensing or registration

Applies

Licence needed (Scotland-wide). All short-term lets (secondary letting, home letting, home sharing) need a licence from the council before taking bookings or guests. Step-by-step licence guide and checklist. Apply or renew through Scottish Borders Council (urgent questions: stllicensing@scotborders.gov.uk).

The council says unlicensed letting is enforced by Police Scotland.

Fees

New and renewal, usually a 3-year licence: £417 (1 to 3 guests), £465 (4 to 6), £537 (7 or more). Variation or change in circumstances £104, duplicate licence £50. The council may charge for inspections after a breach of conditions or a complaint.
