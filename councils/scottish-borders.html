Short-term let rules in Scottish Borders (updated 22 Oct 2026)

Licence needed (3-year licence, £417 to £537 by guest numbers). No control area. No visitor levy proposed.

Licensing or registration

Licence needed (Scotland-wide). All short-term lets (secondary letting, home letting, home sharing) need a licence from the council before taking bookings or guests. Step-by-step licence guide and checklist. Apply or renew through Scottish Borders Council (urgent questions: stllicensing@scotborders.gov.uk). The council says unlicensed letting is enforced by Police Scotland.

Source: source source

Control area / planning

No control area. mygov.scot lists only two in Scotland (all of Edinburgh, and Badenoch & Strathspey in Highland).

Source: source

Visitor levy

No visitor levy proposed. Scottish Borders is not on VisitScotland's list of councils that have introduced, are consulting on, or are engaging on a levy (checked 22 Oct 2026).

Source: source

Fees

New and renewal, usually a 3-year licence: £417 (1 to 3 guests), £465 (4 to 6), £537 (7 or more). Variation or change in circumstances £104, duplicate licence £50. The council may charge for inspections after a breach of conditions or a complaint.
