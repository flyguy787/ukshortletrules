Gwynedd short-let rules: quick answers

Do I need a licence or registration for a short-term let in Gwynedd?

No council licence yet. Wales-wide: if you take bookings of 31 nights or fewer you must register with the Welsh Revenue Authority by 31 Mar 2027 (free, applies even with no council levy). A national licensing scheme is legislated but not in force (default commencement March 2030). Check if you must register.

Do I need planning permission for a short-term let in Gwynedd?

Article 4 direction (C3 to C5/C6) was quashed and is not in force; the Court of Appeal refused appeal on 6 Feb 2026.

Is there a visitor levy (tourist tax) in Gwynedd?

No levy. The council consulted from 20 May to Jul 2026 and says (page updated 1 Oct 2026) it has not made a decision. Any Gwynedd levy could start no earlier than Oct 2027. GOV.WALES lists Cardiff as the only council charging.
