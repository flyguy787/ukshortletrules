Short-term let rules in Stirling (updated 22 Oct 2026)

Last checked 22 Oct 2026: no change logged for this council in our changes log. Get free alerts when it changes.

3% visitor levy from 14 Jun 2027 (bookings made from 1 Jan 2027); Stirling residents and nights beyond 7 refunded. No control area. Licence fees from £113 (home letting/sharing, up to 4) and £283 (secondary letting, up to 4), as published for 2025-26.

Licensing or registration

Applies

Licence needed (Scotland-wide). All short-term lets (secondary letting, home letting, home sharing) need a licence from the council before taking bookings or guests. Step-by-step licence guide and checklist. Stirling Council application steps: email or post the forms to the Private Rented Housing team, then pay once you get a reference number.

Source: source source

🏘

Control area / planning

None found

No control area. mygov.scot lists only two in Scotland (all of Edinburgh, and Badenoch & Strathspey in Highland). Check planning permission before you apply. If the property is in Loch Lomond & The Trossachs National Park, the park authority is the planning authority; you may also need a building warrant for alterations.

Source: source source

💷

Visitor levy

Applies

Approved: 3% (charged before VAT) on paid overnight stays from 14 Jun 2027, for bookings made on or after 1 Jan 2027. Applies all year across the whole council area. Exemptions (Stirling residents on the council tax register, nights beyond the first 7, some disability benefits, carers) are handled by refund to the visitor after the stay. Providers keep 3% of the levy collected.

Source: source source

💰

Fees

As published on the council page (which says these apply 1 Apr 2025 to 31 Mar 2026 and are reviewed each year; 2026-27 figures not shown there). 3-year licence, first-time: home letting and/or sharing £113 (4 people or fewer) / £283 (5 or more); secondary letting £283 / £453. Renewal: £85 / £227 and £227 / £397. Temporary licence or exemption (up to 6 weeks): £91 / £170 and £142 / £227.
