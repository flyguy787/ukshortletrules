Short-term let rules in East Lothian (updated 22 Oct 2026)

Last checked 22 Oct 2026: no change logged for this council in our changes log. Get alerts when it changes.

Licence needed. No control area, but check planning before applying. Draft 5% visitor levy consulted on (26 Jun to 18 Sep 2026); a 27 Oct 2026 council meeting is asked to approve an independent review before any decision.

📋

Licensing or registration

Applies

Licence needed (Scotland-wide). All short-term lets (secondary letting, home letting, home sharing) need a licence from the council before taking bookings or guests. Step-by-step licence guide and checklist. Apply through the East Lothian Council short-term lets page (licensing team: stl@eastlothian.gov.uk, 01620 827664). Applications are advertised by a site notice; anyone can comment within 21 days. The council publishes a public register of licences.

Source: source source

🏘

Control area / planning

None found

No control area: the council says it has not designated any, but will review this over time. You still need to check whether your property needs planning permission (Planning: environment@eastlothian.gov.uk, 01620 827216). If you apply without needed planning permission or a Certificate of Lawful Use or Development, the council will refuse to consider the application; you then have three months to apply for planning permission or a certificate, and the licence fee is not refunded if it is not granted.

Source: source source

💷

Visitor levy

Applies

Not approved. The council consulted on a draft scheme from 26 Jun to 18 Sep 2026: a 5% levy on accommodation charges, all year, with no cap on chargeable nights. The consultation had 530 responses. A report to the full council meeting on 27 Oct 2026 asks councillors to approve an independent review of the economic, social and community effects (including the option of no levy) before further recommendations on whether, and in what form, a levy should go ahead.

Source: source

💰

Fees

Fees are in the council's licensing fees booklet (not summarised here, as we could not open the booklet when we checked on 22 Oct 2026). The provisional licence fee only applies where the accommodation is under construction, not refurbishment. Fees are not refunded if an application is refused or withdrawn.
