Licensing or registration

Registration required

No council licence yet. Wales-wide: if you take bookings of 31 nights or fewer you must register with the Welsh Revenue Authority by 31 Mar 2027 (free, applies even with no council levy).

A national licensing scheme is legislated but not in force (default commencement March 2030). Check if you must register · What the licensing Act will require.

Fees

WRA registration is free. Council tax second-home premium: 150% on top of the standard charge (250% in total), retained for 2026-27 and 2027-28 (decided 23 Oct 2025).

No discount for second homes or holiday homes. Exceptions include Class 5 (occupied caravan pitches and boat moorings) and Class 6 (seasonal homes or holiday lets where year-round occupation is prohibited, for example by a planning condition).
