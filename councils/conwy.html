Short-term let rules in Conwy (updated 22 Oct 2026)

Visitor levy decision deferred by Full Council on 24 Sep 2026. Council tax second-home premium of 150% (250% of the standard bill) kept for 2026-27 and 2027-28.

Licensing or registration

No council licence yet. Wales-wide: if you take bookings of 31 nights or fewer you must register with the Welsh Revenue Authority by 31 Mar 2027 (free, applies even with no council levy). A national licensing scheme is legislated but not in force (default commencement March 2030). Check if you must register.

Control area / planning

No Article 4 direction or local short-let planning control found on the council's site (checked 23 Oct 2026).

Visitor levy

No levy yet. Full Council on 24 Sep 2026 deferred any decision until more evidence and practical experience from levies elsewhere in Wales are available. Its consultation ran 20 May to 17 Jul 2026. Any Welsh levy needs 12 months' notice before it starts.

Source: source source

Fees

WRA registration is free. Council tax second-home premium: 150% on top of the standard charge (250% in total), retained for 2026-27 and 2027-28 (decided 23 Oct 2025). No discount for second homes or holiday homes. Exceptions include Class 5 (occupied caravan pitches and boat moorings) and Class 6 (seasonal homes or holiday lets where year-round occupation is prohibited, for example by a planning condition).
