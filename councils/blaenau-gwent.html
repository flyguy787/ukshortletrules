Licensing or registration

Registration required

No council licence yet. Wales-wide: if you take bookings of 31 nights or fewer you must register with the Welsh Revenue Authority by 31 Mar 2027 (free, applies even with no council levy).

A national licensing scheme is legislated but not in force (default commencement March 2030). Check if you must register · What the licensing Act will require.

Control area / planning

None found

No Article 4 direction or local short-let planning control found (checked 25 Oct 2026). Wales-wide, use classes C3 (main home), C5 (second home) and C6 (short-term let) exist since Oct 2022, and moving between them is permitted development unless an Article 4 direction applies.

Visitor levy

None found

No levy. GOV.WALES lists Cardiff as the only council that will charge the Visitor Levy and lists no councils consulting on it (checked 23 Oct 2026). Any Welsh levy needs a consultation, a full Council decision and 12 months' notice.

Source: source source

💰

Fees

WRA registration is free. Council tax second-home premium: UNVERIFIED for 2026/27, because the sources disagree.

The council's premiums page says a 100% second-home premium (so a double bill) starts on 1 Apr 2026 for homes that are nobody's sole or main residence and are substantially furnished. But the council's own 2026/27 return to the Welsh Government shows no second homes paying a premium, and GOV.WALES says only 20 of the 22 Welsh councils charge one in 2026/27.

Ask the council (Premiums@blaenau-gwent.gov.uk, 01495 363900) which applies before you budget. Long-term empty homes (empty and unfurnished for over 12 months) have paid a premium since 1 Apr 2025: 100% after 1 to 2 years, 200% after 2 to 3 years and 300% after more than 3 years, ignoring any time empty before 1 Apr 2024.

Exceptions: Class 1 and 2 (marketed for sale or let, one year only), Class 3 (annexes), Class 4 (armed forces), Class 5 (occupied caravan pitches and boat moorings), Class 6 (seasonal homes or holiday lets where year-round occupation is prohibited) and Class 7 (job-related homes), plus local exceptions for long-term empty homes only (new buyers for up to 12 months, awaiting an empty-property grant or loan, complex legal issues, awaiting planning permission, and some mixed-use buildings).

Apply with the council's premium exception form.
