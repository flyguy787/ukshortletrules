Short-term let rules in Argyll and Bute (updated 22 Oct 2026)

What changed (2 Oct 2026): Argyll and Bute corrects figures in its control area consultation and extends it by two weeks, to 15 Nov 2026. Source · Get alerts

Consulting (21 Sep to 15 Nov 2026) on a proposed control area for Mull and Iona; Coll and Tiree; Islay, Jura and Colonsay; and Oban, Lorn and the Inner Isles. Not in force. Visitor levy consultation (flat rate per room per night proposed) closed 5 Oct 2026; no decision yet. 3-year licence from £285.30 (home sharing/letting, up to 4 guests) or £398.95 (secondary letting, up to 4 guests).

📋

Licensing or registration

Applies

Licence needed (Scotland-wide). All short-term lets (secondary letting, home letting, home sharing) need a licence from the council before taking bookings or guests. Step-by-step licence guide and checklist. Apply or renew online via the council. The council does not consider temporary exemptions or temporary licences. Public register of licences on argyll-bute.gov.uk.

Source: source source

🏘

Control area / planning

Applies

Proposed, not in force: consultation from 21 Sep to 15 Nov 2026 on a control area covering Mull and Iona; Coll and Tiree; Islay, Jura and Colonsay; and Oban, Lorn and the Inner Isles. No control area is designated yet. The council says outside a control area you don't need proof of planning permission to get a licence, but you must make sure any needed permission is in place; inside a control area, a new change of use of a dwelling to a secondary let would always need planning permission. New buildings, pods and converted garages or sheds for short-term letting will generally need planning permission.

Source: source source

💷

Visitor levy

Applies

Not approved. The council consulted on a revised draft scheme with a flat rate per room per night (allowed by the Visitor Levy (Amendment) (Scotland) Act 2026). The consultation closed 5 Oct 2026; findings go to a future council meeting. Proposed amount: not stated on the council page.

Source: source

💰

Fees

3-year licence, same fee for new and renewal applications. Home sharing or home letting: £285.30 (up to 4 guests), £398.95 (5 to 8), £627.40 (9 to 12), £799.15 (13 to 20), £1,027.65 (21 and over). Secondary letting: £398.95 / £570.60 / £855.95 / £1,141.30 / £1,426.60. Guest capacity includes children under 10. Transfer £97.20, variation £97.20.
