Short-term let rules in Carmarthenshire (updated 22 Oct 2026)

100% council tax premium on second homes (200% bill) since 1 Apr 2025. Holiday lets need free WRA registration by 31 Mar 2027. No visitor levy.

Licensing or registration

No council licence yet. Wales-wide: if you take bookings of 31 nights or fewer you must register with the Welsh Revenue Authority by 31 Mar 2027 (free, applies even with no council levy). A national licensing scheme is legislated but not in force (default commencement March 2030). Check if you must register.

Control area / planning

No Article 4 direction or local short-let planning control found (checked 23 Oct 2026). Wales-wide, use classes C3 (main home), C5 (second home) and C6 (short-term let) exist since Oct 2022, and moving between them is permitted development unless an Article 4 direction applies.

Source: source source

Visitor levy

No levy. GOV.WALES lists Cardiff as the only council that will charge the Visitor Levy and lists no councils consulting on it (checked 23 Oct 2026). Any Welsh levy needs a consultation, a full Council decision and 12 months' notice.

Source: source source

Fees

WRA registration is free. Council tax second-home premium: 100% on top of the standard charge (so double) for all second homes from 1 Apr 2025 (50% from 1 Apr 2024, approved by Council on 8 Mar 2023). Exceptions include Class 5 (occupied caravan pitches and boat moorings) and Class 6 (seasonal homes or holiday lets where year-round occupation is prohibited, for example by a planning condition). Ask about exceptions at counciltaxpremium@carmarthenshire.gov.uk.
