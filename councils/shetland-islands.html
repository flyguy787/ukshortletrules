Licensing or registration

Applies

Licence needed (Scotland-wide). All short-term lets (secondary letting, home letting, home sharing) need a licence from the council before taking bookings or guests. Step-by-step licence guide and checklist. Shetland Islands Council's Environmental Health team runs the scheme: apply through the council's online portal, by email to stl@shetland.gov.uk, or by post to Environmental Health, Gutters' Hut, 7 North Ness, Lerwick ZE1 0LZ. Fees are non-refundable whether or not a licence is granted.

Visitor levy

None found

No levy. Councillors on the Policy and Resources Committee decided against an overnight visitor levy in September 2025 after a feasibility study said the risks outweighed the benefits. The council has been looking at a point-of-entry levy instead, which would need new law.

Fees

2026/27 fees, now set by guest numbers rather than bedrooms. Home sharing or home letting: £210 (1 to 6 guests) / £335 (7 or more).

Secondary letting: £370 / £530. Admin fee £37.
