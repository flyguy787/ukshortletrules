Perth & Kinross short-let rules: quick answers

Do I need a short-term let licence in Perth & Kinross?

Licence needed (Scotland-wide). All short-term lets (secondary letting, home letting, home sharing) need a licence from the council before taking bookings or guests. Step-by-step licence guide and checklist. Public register at pkc.gov.uk/article/22511.

Do I need planning permission for a short-term let in Perth & Kinross?

Proposed, not in force: Highland Perthshire and the northern part of Eastern Perthshire, agreed 17 Jun 2026 and sent to Scottish Ministers. The council page (last modified 2 Jul 2026) still says it awaits their response. Planning press reports Ministers approved it on 12 Oct 2026: UNVERIFIED. Either way, it only takes effect after the council publishes a designation notice with at least 28 days' notice and a start date. Flats already need planning permission to become a short-term let.

Is there a visitor levy (tourist tax) in Perth & Kinross?

Draft scheme with fixed-rate options under statutory consultation from 19 Aug to Wed 11 Nov 2026. No decision yet; Council decides after considering responses. Reported rate of £4 per room per night: UNVERIFIED (not stated on the council page).

How much does a short-term let licence cost in Perth & Kinross?

3-year licence, new or renewal (Policy Statement, 10 Mar 2025). Secondary letting: £350 (1–2 guests), £530 (3–6), £900 (7–10), £1,600 (11+). Home letting/sharing: £175 / £265 / £450 / £800. Variation or transfer £75.
