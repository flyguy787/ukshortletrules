Short-term let rules in Perth & Kinross (updated 21 Oct 2026)

Licence fees, 3-year licence, new or renewal (Policy Statement, 10 Mar 2025). Secondary letting: £350 (1–2 guests), £530 (3–6), £900 (7–10), £1,600 (11+). Home letting/sharing: £175 / £265 / £450 / £800. Variation or transfer £75. Proposed control area (Highland Perthshire + N. Eastern Perthshire) agreed 17 Jun 2026; awaiting Ministers' approval and not in force. Flats already need planning permission to become a short-term let. Visitor levy: draft scheme under consultation since 19 Aug 2026, no decision yet (reported £4 per room per night UNVERIFIED). Public register at pkc.gov.uk/article/22511.

Licensing or registration

Licence needed (Scotland-wide). All short-term lets (secondary letting, home letting, home sharing) need a licence from the council before taking bookings or guests. Step-by-step licence guide and checklist. Public register at pkc.gov.uk/article/22511.
