Short-term let rules in North Ayrshire (updated 22 Oct 2026)

Last checked 22 Oct 2026: no change logged for this council in our changes log. Get free alerts when it changes.

Licence needed (£300 or £480 for a new secondary let, by guest numbers; covers Arran and Cumbrae). No control area. No visitor levy decided yet. Second homes pay double council tax since April 2025.

Licensing or registration

Applies

Licence needed (Scotland-wide). All short-term lets (secondary letting, home letting, home sharing) need a licence from the council before taking bookings or guests. Step-by-step licence guide and checklist. Apply to North Ayrshire Council's licensing team, using its STL licensing checklist to gather your supporting documents, and include the fee. A licence lasts 3 years from the date of issue and is normally renewed for a further 3 years. If you apply to renew before it expires, your licence carries on until the council decides. Processing can take up to 3 months. Where public notification is needed, a site notice is normally displayed for 21 days. This covers the mainland and the islands of Arran and Cumbrae.

🏘

Control area / planning

None found

No control area. mygov.scot lists only two in Scotland (all of Edinburgh, and Badenoch & Strathspey in Highland). North Ayrshire's licensing policy confirms the council does not currently have a short-term let control area and has not adopted an overprovision approach. You may still need planning permission, depending on the property.

Source: source source

💷

Visitor levy

None found

No levy yet. North Ayrshire's 2025 Visitor Management Plan says officers are looking at a possible visitor levy, including stakeholder pre-consultation and an options appraisal, with a report to Cabinet to follow. In January 2026 the Scottish Government said the council had not yet confirmed whether it will go ahead. There is no published scheme, and any Scottish levy needs at least 18 months' notice. Separately, second homes in North Ayrshire (including on Arran and Cumbrae) have paid a 100% council tax premium (double council tax) since 1 April 2025; a let that qualifies for business rates is not charged council tax.

Source: source source source

💰

Fees

Fees from 1 April 2026, by maximum occupancy (up to 4 guests / 5 or more). New licence: secondary let £300 / £480; home sharing or home letting (or both) £150 / £300. Renewal: secondary let £240 / £420; home sharing or letting £135 / £240. Provisional licence: 25% of the new fee when you apply, 75% on confirmation. Temporary licence: 50% of the new fee. Amendment £75, variation £90, transfer £110, replacement licence £60. No refund if refused, withdrawn or granted for a shorter period.
