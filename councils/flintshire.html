Licensing or registration

Registration required

No council licence yet. Wales-wide: if you take bookings of 31 nights or fewer you must register with the Welsh Revenue Authority by 31 Mar 2027 (free, applies even with no council levy).

A national licensing scheme is legislated but not in force (default commencement March 2030). Check if you must register · What the licensing Act will require.

Control area / planning

None found

No Article 4 direction or local short-let planning control found (checked 25 Oct 2026). Wales-wide, use classes C3 (main home), C5 (second home) and C6 (short-term let) exist since Oct 2022, and moving between them is permitted development unless an Article 4 direction applies.

Visitor levy

None found

No levy. GOV.WALES lists Cardiff as the only council that will charge the Visitor Levy and lists no councils consulting on it (checked 23 Oct 2026). Any Welsh levy needs a consultation, a full Council decision and 12 months' notice.

Source: source source

Fees

WRA registration is free. Council tax second-home premium: 100% on top of the standard charge (so double) since 1 Apr 2023 (Council decision 13 Dec 2022), kept for 2026/27 when Council set the tax on 24 Feb 2026.

It applies to substantially furnished homes that are not anyone's sole or main residence, and Flintshire gives no discount on second homes. Long-term empty homes pay a 100% premium after one year, rising from Apr 2026 to 150% after 3 years, 200% after 5 years and 300% after 10 years.

Exceptions include Class 5 (occupied caravan pitches and boat moorings) and Class 6 (seasonal homes or holiday lets where year-round occupation is prohibited, for example by a planning condition). Class 7 (job-related homes) and Classes 1 to 4 (marketed for sale or let for up to a year, annexes, armed forces) also apply.
