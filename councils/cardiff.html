Cardiff short-let rules: quick answers

Do I need a licence or registration for a short-term let in Cardiff?

No council licence yet. Wales-wide: if you take bookings of 31 nights or fewer you must register with the Welsh Revenue Authority by 31 Mar 2027 (free, applies even with no council levy). A national licensing scheme is legislated but not in force (default commencement March 2030). Check if you must register.

Do I need planning permission for a short-term let in Cardiff?

No Article 4 direction listed.

Is there a visitor levy (tourist tax) in Cardiff?

Visitor levy for new bookings for stays from 1 Apr 2027: £1.30 per person per night (75p for shared hostel/dorm rooms and tent pitches).

What fees apply to short-term lets in Cardiff?

WRA registration is free.
