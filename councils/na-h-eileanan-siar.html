Licensing or registration

Applies

Licence needed (Scotland-wide). All short-term lets (secondary letting, home letting, home sharing) need a licence from the council before taking bookings or guests. Step-by-step licence guide and checklist. Comhairle nan Eilean Siar short-term lets pages: send the completed application and fee to stl@cne-siar.gov.uk, any Comhairle office, or by post to Stornoway.

Submit renewals, with everything needed, about 3 months before your licence expires; if a complete renewal is not in before expiry you count as a new operator and cannot take bookings or guests until licensed.

Control area / planning

None found

No control area. mygov.scot lists only two in Scotland (all of Edinburgh, and Badenoch & Strathspey in Highland).

Source: source

💷

Visitor levy

None found

No levy. The Comhairle has paused detailed work on an accommodation-based visitor levy, citing challenges for island businesses, and is looking at other options for funding tourism. It says any formal proposal would be consulted on before councillors consider it.

Source: source source

💰

Fees

3-year licence, 2026/27 fees: a base fee plus £21 per guest. First application: home sharing £189, secondary letting, home letting, or both home sharing and letting £294.

Renewal: home sharing £84, the others £189. Example: a 6-guest secondary let costs £294 + 6 × £21 = £420.

Temporary exemption (up to 6 weeks) £53, transfer £53, variation £53. No refunds once an application is processed.

Source: source source
