Control area / planning

Applies

Badenoch & Strathspey ward is a control area (since 4 Mar 2024). Rest of Highland: none in force.

On 28 Sep 2026 the council's Economy and Infrastructure Committee agreed to submit two proposed control areas to Scottish Ministers: an Inverness City control area (Inverness and surrounding areas) and a Highland Rural control area (several rural areas, including Aird and Loch Ness, Lochaber, Skye and Raasay, and Sutherland; full list in the council's announcement).

They are proposals only. If Ministers approve them, converting a home into a secondary short-term let there would need planning permission; the council says they would not apply retrospectively to existing operators unless permission was already needed, and that start dates will come in a later report (council news, 28 Sep 2026, checked 28 Oct 2026).

Visitor levy

Fees

New or full-licence renewal, by guests (not counting children under 2). Home sharing or home letting: £320 (1 to 2 guests), £390 (3 to 6), £460 (7 to 9), £530 (10 or more).

Secondary letting: £400 / £470 / £540 / £610. Temporary licence 50% of the full fee; variation or change in circumstance £120; duplicate licence £20.

The council may charge for an inspection after a breach of conditions or a complaint. The council's fees page was last modified 14 Aug 2025 (read 26 Oct 2026), so check for a 2026/27 update.
