Fees

New or full-licence renewal, by guests (not counting children under 2). Home sharing or home letting: £320 (1 to 2 guests), £390 (3 to 6), £460 (7 to 9), £530 (10 or more). Secondary letting: £400 / £470 / £540 / £610. Temporary licence 50% of the full fee; variation or change in circumstance £120; duplicate licence £20. The council may charge for an inspection after a breach of conditions or a complaint. The council's fees page was last modified 14 Aug 2025 (read 26 Oct 2026), so check for a 2026/27 update.
