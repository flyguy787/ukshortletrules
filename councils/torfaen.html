Licensing or registration

Registration required

No council licence yet. Wales-wide: if you take bookings of 31 nights or fewer you must register with the Welsh Revenue Authority by 31 Mar 2027 (free, applies even with no council levy). A national licensing scheme is legislated but not in force (default commencement March 2030). Check if you must register · What the licensing Act will require.

Control area / planning

None found

No Article 4 direction or local short-let planning control found (checked 25 Oct 2026). Wales-wide, use classes C3 (main home), C5 (second home) and C6 (short-term let) exist since Oct 2022, and moving between them is permitted development unless an Article 4 direction applies.

Visitor levy

None found

No levy. GOV.WALES lists Cardiff as the only council that will charge the Visitor Levy and lists no councils consulting on it (checked 23 Oct 2026). Any Welsh levy needs a consultation, a full Council decision and 12 months' notice.

Source: source source

💰

Fees

WRA registration is free. No council tax second-home premium: Torfaen is one of only two Welsh councils not charging one in 2026/27 (Welsh Government council tax dwellings return: 0 homes on any premium in Torfaen), and it does not charge a long-term empty premium either. The council says it is considering premiums on second homes and long-term empty homes, is reviewing how many properties would be affected, and that any decision would need public consultation and councillors' approval. Its stated approach for now is to help owners bring empty homes back into use rather than charge a premium. A council's first decision to charge a second-home premium must be made at least a year before the financial year it starts, so check the council's page before buying. Second homes and empty homes get no council tax discount, so a furnished holiday let on council tax pays the standard bill (2026/27 bill notes).
