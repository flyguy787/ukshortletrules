Visitor levy

Applies

5% of the accommodation cost, first 5 consecutive nights, stays from 24 Jul 2026. Quarterly returns via visitorlevy.scot. First returns (stays 24 Jul to 30 Sep 2026) are due 1 to 30 Oct 2026, with payment by 13 Nov 2026. Providers keep 2%.

Fees

2026/27 fees (council fee schedule, valid 2026/27). Secondary letting, by maximum occupancy, 1-year new or renewal: £653 (1 to 3), £1,089 (4 to 5), £2,481 (6 to 10), £3,872 (11 to 15), £5,264 (16 to 20), £5,869 (21 and over); 3-year renewal: £1,053 / £1,489 / £2,881 / £4,272 / £5,664 / £6,869. Home letting, or home sharing and home letting: £120 per occupant (new, 1 year) or £360 per occupant (renewal, 3 years). Home sharing only: £120. Temporary licence or temporary exemption: £250 (1 to 5), £350 (6 to 10), £600 (11 and over); £120 for home sharing. Provisional licence 20% and confirmation 80% of the full fee. Fees are non-refundable. Operating without a licence can mean a fine of up to £2,500.
