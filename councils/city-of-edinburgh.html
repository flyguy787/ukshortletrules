Short-term let rules in City of Edinburgh (updated 22 Oct 2026)

Whole-city control area (planning likely needed for secondary lets). 5% visitor levy from 24 Jul 2026, capped at 5 nights. First levy returns due by 30 Oct 2026, payment by 13 Nov 2026.

Licensing or registration

Licence needed (Scotland-wide). All short-term lets (secondary letting, home letting, home sharing) need a licence from the council before taking bookings or guests. Step-by-step licence guide and checklist.

Control area / planning

Yes: the whole city has been a short-term let control area since 5 Sep 2022. Using a whole dwelling that isn't your main home as a short-term let very likely needs planning permission.

Visitor levy

5% of the accommodation cost, first 5 consecutive nights, stays from 24 Jul 2026. Quarterly returns via visitorlevy.scot. First returns (stays 24 Jul to 30 Sep 2026) are due 1 to 30 Oct 2026, with payment by 13 Nov 2026. Providers keep 2%.
