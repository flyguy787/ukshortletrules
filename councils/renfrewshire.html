Licensing or registration

Applies

Licence needed (Scotland-wide). All short-term lets (secondary letting, home letting, home sharing) need a licence from the council before taking bookings or guests. Step-by-step licence guide and checklist. Apply and pay online through Renfrewshire Council's MyAccount (electronic signatures accepted).

A new application needs a plan of the accommodation (not to scale, showing what the policy requires), buildings insurance and public liability insurance, plus proof of right to work and a criminal record check; a provisional licence is also available.

Display the council's notice at or near the premises for 21 days from the day you apply (objections close 28 days after you apply), then return the notice and a signed certificate of compliance. Police Scotland, Scottish Fire and Rescue, the local councillor, the community council and the council's planning team are consulted.

If there is an objection, the Regulatory Functions Board decides and you can appeal to the Sheriff within 28 days. The council aims to consider applications within 3 months and decide within 9.

A licence lasts three years; apply to renew at least 6 weeks before it expires, or it stops being valid. To change licence type you need a new application, not a variation.

Questions go to licensing.cs@renfrewshire.gov.uk.

Control area / planning

None found

No control area. mygov.scot lists only two in Scotland (all of Edinburgh, and Badenoch & Strathspey in Highland). You may still need planning permission depending on the property; Renfrewshire's planning team checks every application for relevant planning consents.

Visitor levy

None found

No levy yet. On 17 September 2025 the Leadership Board authorised officers to begin formal stakeholder consultation and develop a visitor levy proposition, after an estimate that a 5% levy could raise about £2m a year before costs.

We found no published consultation or scheme since (searched 24 Oct 2026). Any Scottish levy needs at least 18 months' notice.

Fees

Application fees from 1 April 2026, by bedrooms let (1 / 2 / 3 / 4 or more). Home letting, home sharing or both: £291 / £407 / £407 / £407.

Secondary letting: £927 / £1,043 / £1,159 / £1,274. Provisional licence: home £189 / £264 / £264 / £264, secondary £603 / £678 / £754 / £829; confirming it then costs home £213 / £254 / £254 / £254, secondary £435 / £476 / £516 / £557.

Temporary exemption £117, variation £117, material change of circumstances £117, transfer £89, certified duplicate or true copy £48, inspection after a complaint £156.87 an hour.
