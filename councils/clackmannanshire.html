Licensing or registration

Applies

Licence needed (Scotland-wide). All short-term lets (secondary letting, home letting, home sharing) need a licence from the council before taking bookings or guests. Step-by-step licence guide and checklist. Apply online through Clackmannanshire Council (a PDF form can be posted or handed in at Kilncraigs, Alloa, with the fee and supporting documents). The council points applicants to its Short Term Lets Policy, the fire safety checklist, the mandatory licence conditions and its public Short Term Lets Register. Questions go to licensing@clacks.gov.uk.

Control area / planning

None found

No control area. mygov.scot lists only two in Scotland (all of Edinburgh, and Badenoch & Strathspey in Highland). You may still need planning permission, depending on the property, and secondary lets must show their planning status when applying.

Visitor levy

None found

No levy proposed. We found no published visitor levy consultation or scheme for Clackmannanshire (searched 24 Oct 2026), and it is not on the visitorlevy.scot list of councils with approved schemes. In a Scotsman survey of councils, Clackmannanshire said it had no plans to introduce one. Any Scottish levy needs at least 18 months' notice.

Source: source source

💰

Fees

Fees from the council's Register of Charges 2026/27, by occupancy (4 or under / 5 or above). New licence: secondary letting £514.30 / £628.70; home letting or home sharing (or both) £314.30 / £400. Renewal: secondary letting £457.20 / £571.50 (the register labels the lower band "4 or above", which looks like a typo for 4 or under); home letting or sharing £257.20 / £514.30. Variation £75. Fees are non-refundable.

Source: source source
