Visitor levy

Applies

Not approved. Formal consultation on a draft scheme runs from 2 Sep to 25 Nov 2026: 5% of the cost of overnight accommodation only (not food, parking or other extras), with providers proposed to keep 2%.

The council says it has not decided, and full Council is expected to consider it in January 2027 (the consultation page says early 2027). Any Scottish levy needs at least 18 months' notice before it starts.
