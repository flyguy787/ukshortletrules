Short-term let rules in Aberdeenshire (updated 22 Oct 2026)

Licence needed (3-year licence, £364 to £1,006 by guest numbers and licence type). No control area. No visitor levy scheme proposed yet.

Licensing or registration

Licence needed (Scotland-wide). All short-term lets (secondary letting, home letting, home sharing) need a licence from the council before taking bookings or guests. Step-by-step licence guide and checklist. Apply or renew online through Aberdeenshire Council (questions: stl@aberdeenshire.gov.uk). You need one licence per separately bookable self-contained unit; a group of yurts or lodges on the same land can get a multi-unit licence. The council says a garden cabin, a self-contained extension or a converted steading, barn or garage with its own access needs a secondary letting licence. You must display a public notice outside the property; objections can be made within 28 days. Documents to send: EICR (within 5 years), PAT certificate and gas safety certificate (both within 12 months), EPC (secondary lets), and evidence of buildings insurance plus public or property owners liability cover of at least £2m. The council will not process incomplete applications and has up to 9 months to decide. A renewal must be in before the expiry date, or you become a new host and pay the higher fee.

Control area / planning

No control area. mygov.scot lists only two in Scotland (all of Edinburgh, and Badenoch & Strathspey in Highland).

Visitor levy

No scheme proposed yet. The council ran an initial survey from 24 Jun to 22 Jul 2025 (767 responses, 65% not supportive) and said a formal consultation would follow; we found no formal consultation published as of 22 Oct 2026. VisitScotland lists Aberdeenshire as early engagement only.

Source: source source

Fees

Applies until 31 Mar 2027, by maximum guests. New home sharing or letting: £364 (1 to 2), £481.50 (3 to 4), £610 (5 to 9), £727.75 (10 to 15), £909.75 (16+). New secondary letting, and provisional licences for new builds or conversions: £481.50, £642, £813.50, £909.75, £1,006. Renewal home sharing or letting: £331.75, £433.50, £545.75, £652.75, £824. Renewal secondary letting: £433.50, £578, £738.50, £824, £920.25. Duplicate licence £53.50. Variation £107 and transfer £150 (the council page dates these to 31 Mar 2026). Fees are non-refundable.
