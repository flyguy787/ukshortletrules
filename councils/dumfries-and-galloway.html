Licensing or registration

Applies

Licence needed (Scotland-wide). All short-term lets (secondary letting, home letting, home sharing) need a licence from the council before taking bookings or guests. Step-by-step licence guide and checklist. Apply through Dumfries and Galloway Council (shorttermlets@dumgal.gov.uk).

Send a floor plan showing room sizes and bedroom occupancy, stairs or lifts, the use of each room, fire escape routes, any accessible rooms, smoke and heat detectors, and CO detectors if there is an open-flue appliance. Applications go on the public register and anyone can object during the 21-day notice period.

Renewals are every 3 years and the council sends a reminder 3 months before expiry. The council says operating without a licence is a criminal offence, with a fine of up to £2,500 and a one-year ban from applying.

Visitor levy

None found

No levy: plans dropped. After early engagement (29 Sep to 21 Nov 2025), the council's economy and infrastructure committee voted 10 to 8 on 9 Jun 2026 to stop all work on a visitor levy, with no formal consultation.

💰

Fees

3-year licence, by maximum guests. Home sharing and home letting: £324 (up to 6), £361 (7 to 12), £413 (13+).

Secondary letting: £357, £397, £454. The council says new, renewal and provisional applications currently cost the same.

From 1 Apr 2026: confirming a provisional licence £79, transfer £79. Fees are non-refundable.
