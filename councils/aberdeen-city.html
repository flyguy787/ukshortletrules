Short-term let rules in Aberdeen City (updated 22 Oct 2026)

7% visitor levy on the whole stay from 1 Apr 2027 (bookings made from 1 Oct 2026). No control area. Licence fees from £470.40 (home sharing/letting, 1–2 guests) and £492.80 (secondary letting, 1–2 guests), effective 1 Jan 2026. No temporary licences or exemptions granted.

Licensing or registration

Licence needed (Scotland-wide). All short-term lets (secondary letting, home letting, home sharing) need a licence from the council before taking bookings or guests. Step-by-step licence guide and checklist. Apply online: Aberdeen City Council application (one application per property). The council does not grant temporary licences or temporary exemptions.

Source: source source

Control area / planning

No control area. mygov.scot lists only two in Scotland (all of Edinburgh, and Badenoch & Strathspey in Highland). The council says that if the let is a whole dwelling that is not your main home, you may need planning permission as well as a licence, so check before applying.

Source: source source

Visitor levy

Approved: 7% of the accommodation cost on the whole stay, for stays from 1 Apr 2027. Applies to bookings made from 1 Oct 2026 for those stays. Approved by the council's Finance & Resources Committee on 6 Aug 2025 under the Visitor Levy (Scotland) Act 2024.

Source: source source

Fees

Same fee for first-time applications and renewals, effective from 1 Jan 2026. Home sharing and/or home letting: £470.40 (1–2 guests), £705.60 (3–4), £940.80 (5–6), £1254.40 (7–8), £1668.80 (9–12), £3337.60 (13+). Secondary letting: £492.80 / £739.20 / £985.60 / £1310.40 / £1752.80 / £3505.60.
