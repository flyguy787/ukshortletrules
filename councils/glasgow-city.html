Fees

Fees from April 2026, for a 3-year licence, by maximum occupancy applied for. Secondary letting: grant £310 (4 guests or fewer) or £494 (5 or more); renewal £247 / £433.

Home letting, home sharing or both: grant £156 / £341; renewal £93 / £278. Variation or transfer £90; confirming a provisional licence £167.

Pay by bank transfer; fees are non-refundable.
