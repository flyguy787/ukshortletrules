Short-term let rules in West Dunbartonshire (updated 21 Oct 2026)

5% visitor levy on the whole stay from 1 Jul 2027 (bookings made and paid for from 1 Oct 2026). No control area. Licence grant or renewal £929.

Licensing or registration

Licence needed (Scotland-wide). All short-term lets (secondary letting, home letting, home sharing) need a licence from the council before taking bookings or guests. Step-by-step licence guide and checklist. Apply online for grant or renewal; upload a 1:50 layout plan, gas and electrical certificates and the fire safety checklist with the application.

Source: source source

Control area / planning

No control area. mygov.scot lists only two in Scotland (all of Edinburgh, and Badenoch & Strathspey in Highland). Applications are sent to the council's planning department, or to the Loch Lomond & The Trossachs National Park planning authority where appropriate. Include planning permission or a certificate of lawfulness where applicable.

Source: source source

Visitor levy

Approved: 5% (before VAT) on the whole stay, for stays from 1 Jul 2027. Applies to bookings made (and paid for in part or full) from 1 Oct 2026. Providers keep 1.5%.

Source: source source

Fees

Grant and renewal application £929.00. Provisional £697.00, confirmation £232.00, transfer £61.00, Environmental Health inspection £60.00. Fees from 1 Apr 2026.

Source: source source
