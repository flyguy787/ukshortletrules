Licensing or registration

Applies

Licence needed (Scotland-wide). All short-term lets (secondary letting, home letting, home sharing) need a licence from the council before taking bookings or guests. Step-by-step licence guide and checklist. Midlothian Council asks you to pay online first; the payment receipt number and time then go on the main application form.

Questions go to shorttermlet@midlothian.gov.uk. The council links its Short-term Lets Policy (2022), a fire safety checklist and a Legionnaires' disease risk assessment, and publishes a public licence register.

Renew before your licence expires: if you do, you can keep operating until the old licence ends or the council decides, whichever is later. A late renewal is treated as a new application and you must stop taking bookings until it is granted; the council may accept one made within 28 days of expiry on good grounds, case by case.

Operating without a licence can mean a fine of up to £2,500 and a one-year ban from applying.

Control area / planning

None found

No control area. mygov.scot lists only two in Scotland (all of Edinburgh, and Badenoch & Strathspey in Highland). You may still need planning permission depending on the property, and secondary lets must show their planning status when applying.

Source: source

Visitor levy

None found

No levy. Midlothian ran a visitor levy survey in 2024 (24 responses), which the council called inconclusive.

Officers are monitoring Edinburgh, Argyll and Bute and Aberdeen and said Midlothian would reassess a levy in 2026; we found no 2026 decision yet (searched 24 Oct 2026). A 2023 council report estimated a levy might raise about £180k a year against running costs of £190k to £500k.

Any Scottish levy needs at least 18 months' notice.

Source: source source

💰

Fees

Fees from 1 April 2026, the same for a new licence or a renewal. Secondary letting: up to 4 bedrooms / 8 guests £541; 5 to 8 bedrooms / 16 guests £609; 8 to 12 bedrooms / 16 to 24 guests £767; above that £61 per extra room or guest.

Home sharing or home letting: 1 bedroom / 3 guests £247; 2 to 3 bedrooms / 9 guests £290; 4 bedrooms / 9 guests £383; above that £61 per extra room or guest. A material change or licence transfer costs £315 / £368 / £452 (secondary) or £116 / £152 / £221 (home), and a variation £89.

Duplicate licence £37; a visit made necessary by the host's failure £127 an hour.

Source: source
