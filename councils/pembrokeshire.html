Short-term let rules in Pembrokeshire (updated 22 Oct 2026)

Council tax second-home premium of 125% from 1 Apr 2026 (on top of the standard charge), down from 150% in 2025-26. Premium for 2027-28 was due to be set by full Council on 15 Oct 2026; local press reports 125% was kept (UNVERIFIED).

Licensing or registration

No council licence yet. Wales-wide: if you take bookings of 31 nights or fewer you must register with the Welsh Revenue Authority by 31 Mar 2027 (free, applies even with no council levy). A national licensing scheme is legislated but not in force (default commencement March 2030). Check if you must register.

Fees

WRA registration is free. Council tax second-home premium: 125% from 1 Apr 2026, on top of the standard charge (150% in 2025-26, 200% in 2024-25). Exceptions apply. The 2027-28 premium was due to be decided by full Council on 15 Oct 2026, taking effect 1 Apr 2027; local press reports 125% was kept: UNVERIFIED (not yet on the council's premium page).
